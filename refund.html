Our Quality Guarantee & Refund Policy

We hand-pack every order on ice and dispatch it fresh. If something isn't right, we'll make it right — quickly and fairly.

Our promise to you

If your fish arrives spoiled, you receive the wrong items, or your order does not arrive, you are covered. Tell us within 24 hours of delivery and we will resolve it — you choose whether you'd like a replacement on your next delivery or a refund.

What's covered

  • Fish arrived spoiled or in poor condition (reported within 24 hours)
  • You received the wrong items or wrong weight
  • Your order did not arrive

What we ask from you

  • Report within 24 hours of delivery — fresh fish can't be assessed days later
  • Send a clear photo of the item and your order number
  • Please keep the fish until your claim is reviewed

Every claim is reviewed individually and fairly. This protects both you and the fishermen who supply us.

If you change your mind (cancelling or rejecting)

We understand plans change. How a cancellation is handled depends on when you tell us — because we buy your fish fresh, to order:

Before we've sourced your fish — cancel any time for a full refund. Nothing has been bought, so nothing is lost.

After we've sourced & dispatched — the fish has already been bought fresh in your name and sent. If you reject it at this stage, your refund is reviewed case-by-case, and any costs already spent on your order (fish, ice, box, transport) may be deducted. We'll always be fair, and we'll explain any deduction clearly.

This isn't to be difficult — it's because fresh seafood bought for one customer can't simply be returned to the sea. It protects the fair price we pay the Shimoni fishermen.

Prepaid orders

Because we source your fish fresh only after payment, an order not yet dispatched can be cancelled for a full refund at any time. Once the fish is bought and packed for you, the terms above apply.
